The important ones, in detail

Not just logos.

FreeRADIUS + Postgres backend

Managed FreeRADIUS 3.x on our hardened infrastructure. Queries hit our Supabase Postgres via session pooler. You add the NAS in the UI, we push config.

Ruckus SmartZone Called-Station-Id

Parse AA-BB-CC-DD-EE-FF:SSID_NAME to route auth per SSID to per-tenant policies. First-class for WaaS.

MikroTik CoA + PPPoE

Change-of-Authorization for session bouncing after payment / plan change. PPPoE integration lands Q4.

Anthropic Claude

Mira runs on claude-sonnet-4-6. Your data never trains a model. Every prompt is scoped to your tenant.

Zoho Books

Two-way sync of invoices and payments. GL codes mapped per plan. Reseller commissions posted to the right ledger automatically.

Resend SMTP

Transactional email out of the box. Per-tenant SMTP config stored in system_settings — bring your own if you prefer.
