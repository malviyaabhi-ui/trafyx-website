The prepaid engine

Three APIs. One clean flow.

Top up

POST /api/prepaid/topup

Voucher redemption, card payment, or admin credit. Increments balance, records source, updates cycle expiry.

Deduct

POST /api/prepaid/deduct

Called on the billing cycle. Debits the wallet, records the ledger entry, blocks the subscriber if balance goes negative.

Expiry check

GET /api/prepaid/expiry-check

Cron-friendly. Marks expired balances, notifies subscribers, disconnects at threshold. Full audit trail.
