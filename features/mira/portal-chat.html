What Mira can and can't do
on the customer side.

✓ Can do

  • Explain the current bill and cycle
  • Confirm connection state and recent sessions
  • Offer speed test link and interpret result
  • Guide through payment flow
  • Open a ticket on the subscriber's behalf
  • Route a plan-change request into the workflow

✗ Won't do without a human

  • Change the subscriber's plan directly
  • Issue a refund or credit
  • Access another subscriber's data
  • Bypass authentication for anyone
  • Close a ticket without notifying the subscriber
  • Make commitments about SLA or credits
