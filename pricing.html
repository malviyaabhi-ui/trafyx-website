Common questions

Pricing FAQ.

What's a "Mira AI credit"?

One credit = one autonomous Mira action (ticket triage, dunning decision, NOC watch alert, or intelligence query). Unused credits roll over for one month. Overages are billed per credit, or you can upsize your tier.

What's an "active subscriber"?

Anyone in status active or suspended at month-end. Terminated and prospect records don't count.

Can I go over the tier limit?

Yes. Overages are billed at a per-subscriber rate. You'll get a heads-up at 80% of your tier before it happens.

Do you offer self-hosted?

Not out of the box, but Enterprise customers can get a dedicated single-tenant deployment on request.

What about payment gateway fees?

Gateway fees (Telr, PayTabs, Stripe, PayPal, or others) are billed directly by the gateway. We don't take a percentage.

Is there a setup fee?

Starter and Growth: no. Enterprise: dedicated implementation is priced per hour, usually 20–40 hours.
