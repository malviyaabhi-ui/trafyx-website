Changelog

Every version.
Since April 2025.

Trafyx ships weekly. Big things and small. Here's the record.

v1.6.0
Aug 26, 2026

Rebrand + Price Books + Mira Dunning

  • Platform renamed from Provix → Trafyx. New identity, same DB.
  • Price Books module: reseller-specific pricing, override plans, visibility toggles.
  • Mira Dunning: AI-driven collections analyst with preview mode. Suspends only above 90% confidence.
  • Page Guides: collapsible how-to strips on every module.
  • NOC Watch cron ships to production (every 5 min).
  • Changelog page (this one).
v1.5.0
Aug 26, 2026

Mira Intelligence + Subscriber Portal v2

  • Mira Intelligence: staff-facing chat over live business snapshot with insight annotations.
  • Sidebar entry "Mira AI" (was Intelligence), moved directly under Dashboard.
  • Subscriber Portal v2: 5 tabs, live connection banner, Cloudflare speed test, referral code, embedded Mira chat.
  • Announcements table + strip in portal.
v1.4.0
Aug 26, 2026

Mira Agent — the six fronts

  • Ticket triage on /api/mira/analyse + /act + /auto.
  • Whitelisted actions: unblock, block, reactivate, suspend, reset_password, escalate, request_info.
  • 90% confidence threshold, sub-90% auto-escalates.
  • Correctly refused to act on ticket with no subscriber (45% confidence, escalated).
  • Portal chat integration (window.dispatchEvent 'open-mira').
v1.3.0

Prepaid Charging + Electronic Wallet

  • /api/prepaid with topup, deduct, expiry-check endpoints.
  • Wallet UI integrated into subscriber detail, reseller pages, voucher redeem modal.
  • Voucher redemption tops up wallet with preview + cycle expiry display.
  • Supports both existing-subscriber and standalone hotspot redemption.
