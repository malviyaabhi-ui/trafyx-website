In plain language

If you run an internet company, this is your whole back office.

Trafyx bills your customers, activates them on the network, handles their support tickets, chases the ones who don't pay, and watches your infrastructure — all of it, from one screen. And most of the boring stuff runs itself.

Get paid, on time

Invoices go out on schedule. Payments reconcile automatically. Overdue accounts get chased by an AI that knows the difference between a first-time slip and a real delinquent.

Answer support in seconds

A customer opens a ticket. Mira reads their whole history, checks their line, drafts a reply, and either sends it or hands it to a human — before your team even sees it.

Know before customers do

A router goes dark. Mira notices in 5 minutes, opens a critical ticket, tells your NOC channel, and auto-closes it when the router comes back. No one calls the on-call guy.

Mira AI

New

Autonomous ticket handling, dunning decisions, NOC monitoring, and a staff analyst that reads your whole business in plain language.

Multi-tenant WaaS

Run many independent ISP tenants over shared APs. SSID-based routing via Called-Station-Id. Per-tenant portals and billing.

Subscribers

Full lifecycle — pending, active, suspended, terminated. PPPoE, IPoE, hotspot. Bulk import from legacy systems.

Billing & Invoices

Three-step generate flow with duplicate prevention. Postpaid, prepaid, wallet-backed. Native VAT, credit notes.

RADIUS & AAA

Managed FreeRADIUS 3.x reading directly from Postgres. Ruckus, MikroTik, Cisco, Huawei — anything RFC 2865.

Subscriber portal

White-label self-care. Live connection status, usage charts, invoices, tickets, plan changes routed through Mira.

Vouchers & hotspot

Batch generate. SMS delivery. Redeem into a wallet or a standalone hotspot session. Face-value tracking.

Prepaid & wallet

Balance-based charging. Top up, deduct, expiry checks. Voucher redemption previews the cycle before commit.

Helpdesk

SLA tracking, priority, internal notes, reply threads. Every new ticket triggers Mira, who acts or escalates.

Price books

Per-reseller pricing. Override prices, hide plans. Enforced at invoicing and in the subscriber portal.

NOC & Intelligence

Live session monitoring. Mira NOC Watch auto-creates critical tickets on outages, auto-resolves on recovery.

Resellers

Credit limits, balances, activation, and per-reseller price books. Their own login, their own plan visibility.
